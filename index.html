BostonBIAS

BostonBias is a society for all bioimage analysts and bioimage analysis enthusiasts based in Massachusetts. We welcome all career stages and levels of expertise. Joining BostonBias should feel like joining a supportive lab!

    What We Do

    • Monthly Zoom meetings
    • Methods presentations
    • Slack channel for discussions
    • Mailing list with occasional announcements
    • Journal clubs

      Anyone in Massachusetts with an interest in Bioimage Analysis can sign up! BostonBias is free and low-to-no time investment.
