BostonBias

BostonBias (Massachusetts Bioimage analysis society) is a free society for all bioimage analysts and bioimage analysis enthusiasts based in Massachusetts. We welcome all career stages and levels of expertise. Our sole goal is to connect Massachusetts-based people with an interest in bioimage analysis.

    What We Do

    • Monthly Zoom meetings
    • Methods presentations
    • Slack channel for discussions
    • Mailing list with occasional announcements
    • Journal clubs
    • Occasional in-person meetings
